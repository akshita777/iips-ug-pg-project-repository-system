# PRODUCT.md

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Students (BCA/MCA) at IIPS DAVV, Indore tracking their longitudinal projects (Semesters 1-10).
- **Secondary:** Guides (Faculty), Evaluators, Coordinators, and Administrators.

## Brand Positioning
A professional, academic, and structured platform that brings transparency, version control (via GitHub), and organized assessment to the academic project lifecycle.

## Voice and Tone
- **Professional & Academic:** Clear, unambiguous, and formal.
- **Encouraging & Constructive:** Focusing on student growth and clear feedback.
- **Direct & Action-Oriented:** For tasks like "Submit Project", "Approve Allocation", and "Review PR".

## Core Constraints & Principles
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js 15, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui.
- **Authentication:** GitHub OAuth (NextAuth).
- **Accessibility:** Must adhere to WCAG standards (using standard shadcn accessibility features).
- **Simplicity:** Minimize clutter; present data (like project lists and reviews) in clear, sortable tables and concise cards.
